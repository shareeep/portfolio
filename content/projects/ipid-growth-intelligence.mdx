## Project facts

This Final Year Project explores how an agentic AI workflow can support B2B fintech growth intelligence. The team designed a system that brings fragmented account, market, regulatory, and go-to-market signals into a cited, reviewable research flow.

The workflow covers source discovery, signal extraction, claim-level citation, human review, and approved export. Research runs retain source cards, run logs, reviewer feedback, and reusable context so business outputs can be traced back to their evidence.

## GTM Engineering at heart

The project is a GTM Engineering problem: it connects research automation with the operating controls needed to produce useful account research and account-based marketing briefs. The goal is not autonomous publishing. It is to shorten the path from scattered evidence to a grounded draft that a person can inspect, correct, and approve.

## Engineering challenges

### Keeping claims grounded

Useful briefs combine many source types, but every synthesized claim needs a visible evidence trail. The workflow therefore treats citation and review state as part of a claim rather than as decoration added after generation.

```text
for source in approved_sources:
    evidence = discover_and_extract(source)
    signals = classify_relevant_signals(evidence)

for claim in draft_brief(signals):
    claim.citations = find_supporting_evidence(claim, signals)
    claim.status = "cited" if claim.citations else "needs-review"

publish_only(review_and_approve(claims))
```

This pseudocode is illustrative. It describes the engineering shape of the workflow without reproducing proprietary implementation details.

### Managing freshness and uncertainty

Market and regulatory evidence changes over time. Evidence therefore needs timestamps, reason codes, and explicit review states so stale or weakly supported material cannot look equivalent to current, cited material.

```text
if evidence.is_stale() or evidence.confidence < review_threshold:
    claim.status = "needs-review"
    claim.reason = explain_uncertainty(evidence)
else:
    claim.status = "cited"
```

### Protecting sponsor data

The design applies access controls, data minimisation, review trails, and an explicit ban on autonomous external publishing. These constraints make human approval a system boundary rather than an optional step.

## Outcome

The project demonstrates how source discovery, retrieval, generation, and review can be composed into a GTM workflow where evidence remains inspectable. The emphasis is on the engineering challenges and controls behind grounded business outputs, not on displaying the underlying product.
